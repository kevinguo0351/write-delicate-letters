## 1. What “细腻” means

Treat delicacy as accuracy at a small scale. A delicate sentence notices the part most people skip: the hand that straightens an already straight napkin, the call that ends before either person says they miss the other, the umbrella left open in the hallway because someone expected to leave again.

Aim for four simultaneous qualities:

- **Specific** — tied to this relationship, not “all relationships.”
- **Restrained** — the sentence does not beg the reader to feel.
- **Revising** — present understanding changes the meaning of a past scene.
- **Generous** — the reader retains freedom; affection is not leverage.

## 2. Eight reusable craft moves

### A. Let an object carry history

Choose an ordinary object already present in the relationship: a thermos, bus ticket, cracked bowl, old voicemail, grocery list, key, scarf, or receipt. Show it first as itself. Let its emotional meaning emerge later.

Weak: “你一直很关心我。”

Stronger pattern: describe the object or action; then reveal what the writer understands it meant.

### B. Write the unperformed gesture

Notice what almost happened: a hand lifted and lowered, a message typed and deleted, a door left unlocked, food kept warm past midnight. Unfinished actions often hold more feeling than declarations.

### C. Use delayed interpretation

Separate event from meaning:

1. show what happened;
2. admit what the writer thought then;
3. name what the writer understands now.

This creates emotional movement without manufacturing plot.

### D. Pair two truths that seem incompatible

Use contradiction to make feeling dimensional:

- gratitude can contain grief;
- leaving can protect what was loved;
- a person can be both the wound's witness and part of its healing;
- an apology can ask for nothing.

Keep the pair grounded in the relationship. Do not turn every sentence into a paradox.

### E. Move between body and thought

Anchor abstract feeling in bodily evidence: dry throat, cold fingertips, a shoulder relaxing, holding breath before opening a message. Use one exact sensation, not a catalogue of symptoms.

### F. Place plain speech beside lyric speech

Follow an image with a simple human sentence. Example pattern:

> [image or scene]. 我那时其实只是很害怕。

The plain sentence makes the lyrical one believable.

### G. Return agency to the recipient

Use language such as “你不必回复,” “我不是来改写结局,” or an equivalent fitted to context only when sincere. Do not hide a request inside a blessing.

### H. End by changing an earlier detail

Return to the opening object, weather, sound, or action. Let it mean something slightly different now. Stop before explaining the symbol.

## 3. Emotional pacing

Use a rising curve, not continuous intensity:

| Movement | Emotional temperature | Typical material |
|---|---:|---|
| Opening | 2/5 | small fact, hesitation, ordinary scene |
| Recognition | 3/5 | detail plus delayed understanding |
| Core admission | 5/5 | the sentence that could not be said before |
| Release | 3/5 | boundary, freedom, or quiet gratitude |
| Ending | 2/5 | image, action, or one plain sentence |

After a high-intensity sentence, lower the temperature. A letter that stays at 5/5 becomes persuasion or performance.

## 4. Anti-AI revision

Search for and replace these patterns unless the context truly earns them:

- “在人生的旅途中” and other universal preambles;
- “愿你眼里有光、心中有爱” and interchangeable blessings;
- “你就像一束光” without a specific event showing what changed;
- three or more parallel “是……也是……” clauses;
- repeated weather, star, sea, season, flower, and light imagery from unrelated metaphor families;
- every paragraph ending in a quotable sentence;
- excessive em dashes, rhetorical questions, and exclamation marks;
- telling the reader exactly how moving the memory is;
- diagnosing the recipient's inner life as fact;
- using vulnerability to demand a reply, forgiveness, or return.

Run the replaceability test: if a paragraph can be pasted into a stranger's birthday card unchanged, rewrite it with relational evidence.

Run the breath test: read the letter aloud. Break any sentence that cannot be spoken naturally in one breath unless the length has a deliberate accumulative effect.

Run the residue test: remove the final sentence. If nothing is lost, the ending was explaining instead of resonating. If the entire meaning disappears, the earlier paragraphs did not earn the ending.

## 5. Scoring rubric

Score each dimension 0–2. Revise any letter scoring below 12/16 or scoring 0 in truthfulness, agency, or specificity.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Specificity | interchangeable | one generic scene | distinctive relational evidence |
| Truthfulness | invented certainty | plausible but assumed | bounded by known facts |
| Emotional movement | static | one turn | clear, earned shift |
| Restraint | melodramatic | mixed | image and silence carry weight |
| Rhythm | uniform | some variation | spoken, breathable, intentional |
| Imagery | decorative/mixed | partly grounded | coherent and scene-bound |
| Agency | pressures recipient | ambiguous | grants genuine freedom |
| Ending | slogan/explanation | competent | quiet return with residue |
