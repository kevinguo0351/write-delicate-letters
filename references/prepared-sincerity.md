# 郑重成章：把真心写成一封准备过的信

## Purpose

Use this overlay when the writer already has abundant sincere material but the draft reads like a live chat, diary, project log, or anxious stream of consciousness. The goal is not to sound formal for its own sake. The goal is to make the recipient feel: **这封信只写给我，而且写信的人认真选择过每一句。**

This overlay can sit on any style variant, for example `物件回声 × 郑重成章` or `灵魂同路 × 郑重成章`. It is not a tenth route.

## Preserve, remove, add

| Preserve | Remove or sharply limit | Add |
|---|---|---|
| dates, places, gestures, work and life particulars | `emm`, `哈哈`, emojis, launch cues | one intentional opening image |
| remembered promises and the recipient's exact concerns | repeated `开心/喜欢/爱你/为什么` | one narrative spine |
| care translated into labor or preparation | stacked parentheses and multiple `PS` notes | delayed interpretation |
| honest self-correction and responsibility | live reasoning, reassurance-seeking, self-defense | one earned core sentence |
| private objects, jokes, projects, rituals | a daily log of everything that happened | a quieter final action or image |

Do not polish away awkward truth. Polish away the writer's need to explain every step of arriving at it.

## The six-pass conversion

### 1. Name one emotional job

Complete this sentence: “After reading, I want the recipient to know ______.” Keep one answer. Move other valid emotions into later letters.

### 2. Build a private evidence ledger

Extract facts under four headings:

- **Moment:** one scene the recipient will recognize immediately.
- **Continuity:** a date, promise, repeated ritual, or project that proves duration.
- **Cost:** effort, attention, restraint, or repair the writer actually gave.
- **Recognition:** what the writer now understands about the recipient or the relationship.

Choose at most three facts for a short letter and five for a long one. Abundance belongs in the archive; selection belongs in the letter.

### 3. Choose one carrier

Select one dominant carrier for the emotion:

- an object that changes meaning;
- one scene revisited from the present;
- a shared work or creative problem;
- a promise whose meaning has matured;
- one ordinary habit that quietly proves attachment.

Do not let chronology become the structure. “上午做了什么、下午做了什么” is source material, not an emotional arc.

### 4. Delay the declaration

Let the evidence arrive before the claim. Instead of repeating “我很爱你,” show what was remembered, prepared, changed, or protected. Use the explicit declaration once, near the peak, after it has been earned.

If the relationship is intentionally undefined, preserve “我爱你” once only when the user clearly wants those exact words. Immediately separate the declaration from labels, reciprocity, exclusivity, or escalation. Otherwise ask whether the emotional peak should remain “爱” or be rendered as “珍惜/在意”; do not decide a relationship boundary through polishing.

### 5. Compress and compose

Aim to retain roughly 35–55% of the raw material. Merge repeated reasons. Turn questions asked for reassurance into statements the writer can own. Replace self-commentary with responsibility.

Examples of the transformation:

- “你是不是不理我了，我知道你在忙但还是好想问为什么” → “那段沉默让我不安；这是我的感受，不该变成向你追索答案的压力。”
- “我准备了好多好多东西，真的很用心” → name one item, one choice, and why it belongs to the recipient.
- “我一直都很爱你，真的” → keep one plain declaration after the evidence.

### 6. End one beat early

End with a concrete action, placement, or image. Do not summarize the moral of the letter. Do not append advice, another confession, or a final request for reassurance.

## Recommended architecture

For a 600–1000 Chinese-character impressive letter:

1. **Composed entrance (10–15%)** — one date, object, or scene; no thesis.
2. **Selected evidence (35–40%)** — two or three relationship-specific facts.
3. **Late recognition (20–25%)** — what those facts mean now.
4. **Core sentence (5–10%)** — one plain, unmistakable sentence.
5. **Freedom and afterimage (15–20%)** — no hidden demand; return to the opening carrier.

Default texture: 65% lived detail, 25% reflection, 10% imagery. Literary weight should come from arrangement and precision before metaphor.

## Calibration checks

Before returning the letter, verify:

- Could the recipient identify at least two facts that belong only to this relationship?
- Is there exactly one main emotional job and one dominant image or object?
- Did the strongest sentence arrive after evidence?
- Are there any fillers, drafting notes, stacked parentheses, or repeated declarations left?
- Does the prose sound revised without sounding ceremonial, archaic, or borrowed?
- Does the ending stop without asking the recipient to comfort the writer?
- If “爱” appears in an undefined relationship, did the user authorize that word and did the letter protect the existing boundary?

If the letter is beautiful but could be addressed to someone else, restore specificity. If it is sincere but reads like a transcript, select harder and explain less.
