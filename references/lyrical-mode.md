## 1. Separate the transcript layers

When the user supplies voice-transcribed writing, classify each segment before learning from it:

| Layer | Signals | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Intended letter | direct address, sustained imagery, relational statements | analyze and preserve the underlying function |
| Teaching commentary | explanation of why a line works, references to “今天写什么” or an audience | extract the craft principle, not the wording |
| Performance cue | English launch words, “开始/停,” audience address | discard |
| False start | repeated words, self-correction, abandoned syntax | keep only if the correction creates deliberate intimacy |
| ASR noise | broken names, impossible word combinations, lost punctuation | infer cautiously or omit; never build a rule from corruption |

Do not paste source lines into the skill. Convert repeated behavior into transferable instructions and write new examples.

## 2. Build a private world

Lyrical letters often make the relationship feel as though it has its own calendar, climate, room, or book. Establish that world in the opening, then remain inside it.

Use one of these functions:

- turn a month into a private coordinate rather than a date;
- make weather express distance, hesitation, or return;
- treat an ordinary object as a rule of the relationship;
- let a room, road, book, garden, or station contain the emotional history.

Original micro-example:

> 四月在我们之间不是月份，更像一张被反复折叠的车票。

Follow the image quickly with a relational fact. The private world must belong to these two people, not to romance in general.

## 3. Control the image field

Choose one semantic field and list 4–6 related nouns or verbs before drafting. Use only 2–3 of them in a short letter.

| Field | Possible vocabulary | Suitable emotions |
|---|---|---|
| Season / weather | thaw, wind, rain line, frost, late light | change, uncertainty, renewal |
| Book / language | margin, preface, annotation, binding, erased line | misunderstanding, memory, unfinished speech |
| Room / architecture | threshold, key, window, lamp, wall | intimacy, avoidance, boundary |
| River / movement | bank, current, ferry, bend, silt | time, separation, return |
| Sewing / repair | seam, knot, loose thread, patch | injury, care, repair |

Do not mix unrelated fields merely to make every line quotable. If the letter begins inside a room, do not suddenly end among galaxies unless the user's real scene earns that move.

## 4. Use address as rhythm

Repeat the recipient's name or “亲爱的” to reopen contact at emotional turns, not as decoration.

Useful placements:

1. once near the beginning to establish intimacy;
2. once before the hardest admission;
3. optionally once before release or farewell.

In a 400–700 character letter, three direct addresses are usually enough. Vary the sentence that follows each address: observation → admission → boundary or blessing.

## 5. Carry conflict without flattening it

Create dimensional feeling by holding two truths in the same image field:

- admiration and resentment;
- desire for closeness and the need to leave;
- gratitude for what happened and refusal to repeat it;
- hope and disbelief.

Use a paired turn once, then explain it with behavior.

Original pattern:

> 我喜欢你把窗打开，也恨你总在风来以前先关掉灯。

The next sentence must name the real interaction: unanswered questions, repeated withdrawal, care taken for granted, or a promise not kept. Do not diagnose the recipient from afar. Describe patterns and their effect on the writer.

Let anger remain legible. “克制” does not mean erasing the grievance; it means refusing insults, mind-reading, and theatrical punishment.

## 6. Shape the ending

Choose one ending function:

- **Return** — revisit the opening image with changed meaning.
- **Self-placement** — locate the writer inside the image without defining the recipient.
- **Boundary** — perform one clear action that closes or changes the relationship.
- **Modest continuation** — offer a small future action rather than an eternal promise.

Original micro-examples:

> 我把最后一把钥匙放回桌上；门外的夜，我会自己走完。

> 下一次降温，我会先问你冷不冷，不再只等你把外套递来。

Stop after the image or plain action. Do not add a universal moral.

## 7. Density limits

Use these ratios as editing guides, not mechanical quotas:

| Register | Relational evidence | Imagery / reflection | Aphoristic lines |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Grounded | about 70% | about 30% | 0–1 |
| Hybrid | about 55% | about 45% | 1–2 |
| Lyrical | at least 40% | at most 60% | no more than 1 per 150–200 Chinese characters |

After two lyrical sentences, place one plain sentence. After one abstract claim, provide one behavior, object, or remembered line.

## 8. Final checks

- Can the image field be named in one phrase?
- Are at least two details unique to this relationship?
- Does each repeated address start a new emotional function?
- Is the grievance stated through behavior rather than diagnosis?
- Does one plain sentence reveal the person beneath the prose?
- Does the ending return, act, or release instead of summarize?
- In a breakup or farewell, does the ending avoid advising the recipient how to change?
- Would replacing the recipient's name with another name break the letter? If not, personalize again.
