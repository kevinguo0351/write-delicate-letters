## 1. How to use the library

This is a mechanism library, not an author-imitation menu. It combines transferable moves found across user-supplied contemporary voice transcripts, modern Chinese correspondence, historical farewell letters, and public-domain European letters. Never label an output “某某风.”

When style is undecided, choose exactly three context-fit variants. Prefer one from each family unless the user's boundary makes a family unsuitable. The three choices must differ in where emotion is carried:

- **赤诚直给** carries emotion in voice, admission, humor, and ordinary life.
- **含蓄映照** carries emotion in scene, object, season, and delayed interpretation.
- **深情思辨** carries emotion in contradiction, shared thought, time, and self-knowledge.

Do not make the user choose from all nine variants.

The nine variants determine **where emotion is carried**. Registers determine imagery density. The optional **郑重成章** overlay determines how thoroughly raw material is selected, composed, and revised. It may be applied after any route; do not list it as a competing tenth variant.

## 2. Three families and nine variants

### Family A — 赤诚直给

#### A1. 俏皮直白

- **Effect:** warm, vivid, close; the writer sounds brave enough to say love without becoming solemn.
- **Mechanisms:** plain address; one bold admission; self-deprecating correction or private joke; one small real-world detail after the strongest line.
- **Best for:** confession, anniversary, reunion, ordinary affection.
- **Avoid:** stand-up-comedy density, teasing an insecurity, or hiding responsibility inside charm.

#### A2. 日常知己

- **Effect:** the recipient feels loved as a whole person and thinking companion, not merely adored.
- **Mechanisms:** move naturally among meals, work, books, worries, decisions, and affection; answer something the recipient once said; let practical care prove intimacy.
- **Best for:** long-term partners, spouses, close friends, encouragement.
- **Avoid:** a reading list disguised as a love letter or abstract praise of the recipient's mind.

#### A3. 炽热誓言

- **Effect:** concentrated and ceremonial; suitable when the relationship has genuinely earned a high emotional temperature.
- **Mechanisms:** short clauses; controlled repetition; a three-step rise from present obstacle to chosen commitment; one concrete fact to keep the vow inhabitable.
- **Best for:** wedding, long separation, grave illness, decisive commitment.
- **Avoid:** “all or nothing” pressure, possession, threats of self-harm, or promises the writer cannot keep.

### Family B — 含蓄映照

#### B1. 时令寄情

- **Effect:** soft and lingering; the relationship seems to have its own private climate.
- **Mechanisms:** open with date, weather, or seasonal threshold; turn it into a relational coordinate; repeat the address at emotional turns; hold two truths in one coherent image field.
- **Best for:** late love letters, unresolved affection, seasonal remembrance, gentle breakup.
- **Avoid:** generic spring/rain/flower decoration or treating weather as proof of the recipient's psychology.

#### B2. 物件回声

- **Effect:** restrained but piercing; the reader recognizes love through something ordinary.
- **Mechanisms:** show one object without explaining it; reveal its history later; return to it in the ending with altered meaning.
- **Best for:** gratitude, apology, family letters, remembrance, quiet farewell.
- **Avoid:** inventing a symbolic object or explaining the symbol twice.

#### B3. 山水留白

- **Effect:** spacious, calm, and indirect; the unsaid remains audible.
- **Mechanisms:** let road, river, distance, light, room, or landscape enter before the confession; use parallel scene and feeling rather than explicit equation; end one beat early.
- **Best for:** long-distance love, admiration, remembrance, dignified release.
- **Avoid:** antique diction, vague scenery, or removing the actual relationship from the letter.

### Family C — 深情思辨

#### C1. 灵魂同路

- **Effect:** intimate and intelligent; love appears as shared attention to the world.
- **Mechanisms:** begin from a question, book, work problem, or belief the two people have discussed; show how the recipient changed the writer's way of seeing; return from thought to one lived action.
- **Best for:** intellectual partners, friendship, admiration, long relationships.
- **Avoid:** lecturing, displaying knowledge, or making the recipient a muse instead of a person.

#### C2. 清醒自白

- **Effect:** vulnerable without losing judgment; contradiction becomes evidence of honesty.
- **Mechanisms:** state what the writer wanted to believe; correct it with observed behavior; distinguish love from what love cannot excuse; place a boundary in plain language.
- **Best for:** conflict, repair, avoidant dynamics, still-loving breakup.
- **Avoid:** diagnosing attachment style, romanticizing anxiety, or using insight to win the argument.

#### C3. 命运告别

- **Effect:** grave, tender, and responsible; private love is held beside time, duty, mortality, or irreversible change.
- **Mechanisms:** alternate intimate particulars with the larger circumstance; name the cost imposed on the recipient; preserve their freedom; make the ending an action or entrusted wish.
- **Best for:** bereavement, final farewell, migration, irreversible separation, letters to one's future self.
- **Avoid:** borrowing tragic grandeur for an ordinary disagreement or turning sacrifice into moral debt.

## 3. Route selection by emotional job

| Emotional job | Strong candidates | Usually avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Confession / late love letter | 俏皮直白 · 时令寄情 · 灵魂同路 | 命运告别 unless stakes are real |
| Long-term affection | 日常知己 · 物件回声 · 灵魂同路 | continuous high-intensity vows |
| Gratitude / family | 日常知己 · 物件回声 · 山水留白 | decorative romance imagery |
| Apology / repair | 物件回声 · 清醒自白 · 日常知己 | charm that dilutes responsibility |
| Comfort / encouragement | 日常知己 · 山水留白 · 灵魂同路 | forced lessons and certainty |
| Difficult love / breakup | 时令寄情 · 清醒自白 · 物件回声 | 俏皮ness that trivializes pain |
| Final or irreversible farewell | 山水留白 · 清醒自白 · 命运告别 | reunion bait or advice to change |

## 4. A/B/C menu format

Return only a compact menu before drafting:

> **A｜[letter type] × [variant]**<br>
> 读感：[one sentence]. 结构：[scene] → [turn] → [ending action].
>
> **B｜[letter type] × [variant]**<br>
> 读感：[one sentence]. 结构：[scene] → [turn] → [ending action].
>
> **C｜[letter type] × [variant]**<br>
> 读感：[one sentence]. 结构：[scene] → [turn] → [ending action].
>
> 选 A / B / C；也可以说“混合 A 的口吻和 B 的结构”。

Do not write three sample paragraphs. The menu should make the choice easy without spending the user's real material prematurely.

If the emotional job itself is ambiguous, let the three routes differ in both job and style, such as “修复 × 物件回声,” “坦白 × 清醒自白,” and “告别 × 山水留白.”

## 5. Composition rules

A chosen variant changes the carrier of emotion, not the truth requirements. Every finished letter still needs:

- at least two relationship-specific facts;
- one clear emotional movement;
- one plain sentence near the emotional peak;
- recipient agency and the requested boundary;
- original wording and imagery.

When 郑重成章 is active, also require visible compositional intent: one narrative spine, no diary chronology, one earned core sentence, and an ending that arrives before the writer starts explaining again.

Mix at most two variants. Name a dominant variant and borrow only one mechanism from the secondary variant. Do not average all styles into generic “beautiful prose.”

## 6. Source traditions and originality boundary

The library draws high-level lessons from these traditions:

- **Contemporary voice-led lyrical letters supplied by the user:** private seasons, recurring address, coherent metaphor fields, contradiction, and ending through self-placement. Because the referenced creator is living, never reproduce signature phrases or provide a style clone.
- **Wang Xiaobo–Li Yinhe and Zhu Shenghao–Song Qingru correspondence:** direct admission, humor, intellectual companionship, ordinary topics, private continuity, and self-correction. Use only mechanisms; do not reuse famous lines or modern edited text.
- **Shen Congwen–Zhang Zhaohe and Lu Xun–Xu Guangping correspondence:** distance, daily circumstances, mutual reading, gradual change in address and relational position, and affection carried beside public life. Do not romanticize persistent pursuit, teacher–student power imbalance, or a recipient's reluctance.
- **Lin Juemin's farewell letter:** the collision of intimate detail, irreversible duty, responsibility, and the recipient's future. Reserve this gravity for genuinely high-stakes contexts.
- **Keats's letters to Fanny Brawne:** bodily sensation, room and weather, longing, jealousy, illness, and mortality held together. Preserve vulnerability but remove coercive possession.
- **Beethoven's “Immortal Beloved” letter:** repetition, compression, absolute intensity, and time-scale endings. Ground intensity in reality and keep the recipient free.
- **Rilke–Lou Andreas-Salomé and Kafka–Milena correspondence:** love as intellectual witness, self-examination, distance, and the letter itself as a problem of contact. Do not aestheticize anxiety or control.

All few-shot examples must remain newly written. Avoid modern translations, edited passages, viral quotations, and disputed quotations as source material.
